# ⚒️ ModernKit

**Bringing newer Minecraft items into older Minecraft versions.**

![ModernKit](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/GItUser1928307/modernkit-source-code/main/images/modernkit-modrinth-256.png)

Ever wanted to experience some of Minecraft's newer items without leaving your favorite older versions behind?

**ModernKit** aims to bridge that gap by introducing items and equipment inspired by newer Minecraft updates into older versions, bringing new possibilities to your gameplay.

Whether you're smashing enemies with a Mace, launching Wind Charges, charging into battle with a Spear, mining bedrock in survival, or equipping yourself with Emerald and Rainbow gear, ModernKit brings more tools and equipment to your Minecraft world.

---

## ✨ What's Included?

### ⚔️ Weapons & Combat

- **Mace** — Fall from a height and land a powerful smash attack. The longer the fall, the more damage you deal. A successful smash cancels your fall damage, knocks back nearby mobs, and plays impact effects.
- **Spears** — Six variants: wooden, stone, golden, iron, diamond, and netherite. Jab with a quick attack, or hold to charge into enemies. Includes first- and third-person animations.
- **Lunge Enchantment** — A spear-exclusive enchantment (levels I to III).
- **Mob Kabob Advancement** — Hit five mobs with a single charge attack.
- **Mace Enchantments** (new in v1.5.6) — The four modern mace enchantments, backported:
  - **Density** (I–V) — Adds 0.5 extra smash damage per block fallen per level. Found at the enchanting table; exclusive with Sharpness, Smite and Bane of Arthropods.
  - **Breach** (I–IV) — Mace hits make the target's armor 15% less effective per level, including natural mob armor, but never Protection enchants or the Resistance effect. Found at the enchanting table; exclusive with Sharpness, Smite and Bane of Arthropods.
  - **Wind Burst** (I–III) — A successful smash launches you back into the air (about 8 blocks per level), letting you chain smash attacks. The burst explosion also knocks back nearby mobs and presses buttons, levers, wooden doors and fence gates, rings bells and breaks chorus flowers, like a Wind Charge — and even mobs like pouncing foxes holding a Wind Burst mace can activate it. Blast Protection reduces the launch. Loot-only treasure enchantment, found in Bastion Treasure and End City chests.
  - **Swift Sneak** (I–III) — Sneaking speed becomes 30% + 15% of walking speed per level (75% at level III). Loot-only treasure enchantment, found in Stronghold Library and Simple Dungeon chests.

### 🌪️ Wind & Breeze Items

- **Wind Charge** — A throwable projectile with its own sounds.
- **Breeze Rod** — Used to craft Wind Charges and the Mace, and to repair the Mace.
- **Heavy Core** — The crafting component at the heart of the Mace.

### ⛏️ Tools

- **Bedrock Pickaxe** — Mine bedrock in survival mode! Bedrock drops itself when mined.

### 💚 Emerald Equipment

A complete equipment collection crafted using Emeralds.

- **Emerald Sword** — An emerald-themed weapon.
- **Emerald Pickaxe** — An emerald-themed mining tool.
- **Emerald Axe** — An emerald-themed axe.
- **Emerald Shovel** — An emerald-themed digging tool.
- **Emerald Armor** — A complete armor set consisting of a Helmet, Chestplate, Leggings, and Boots, with real 3D shaped pieces such as a helmet crest, cheek guards, shoulder pauldrons, knee plates, and boot cuffs.
- **Refined Armor Textures** — Updated in v1.5.2 with a cleaner, more polished appearance. The unwanted gold-colored top layer has been removed from the Emerald Armor textures.
- Emerald equipment uses the same standard crafting shapes as Rainbow equipment, with Emeralds replacing Rainbow Ingots.

### 🌈 Rainbow Set

The most powerful gear in ModernKit, with animated rainbow icons.

- **Rainbow Sword** — Massive damage and a very fast attack speed.
- **Rainbow Pickaxe** — Breaks almost anything, obsidian included, nearly instantly.
- **Rainbow Axe** — A powerful rainbow-themed tool.
- **Rainbow Shovel** — A rainbow-themed digging tool.
- **Rainbow Armor** (Helmet, Chestplate, Leggings, Boots) — Maximum protection and toughness, and full knockback immunity when you wear the whole set. New in v1.5.3: a full 3D armor model with a tall helmet crest, wing fins, shoulder spikes, forearm bracers, hip guards, ankle fins, and a chest gem.
- **Rainbow Ingot** — The crafting and repair material for the set.
- Every Rainbow item is fireproof, enchantable, and has enormous durability.

| Breeze Rod | Blaze Rod + Phantom Membrane |
| Heavy Core | Netherite Ingot surrounded by 4 Obsidian |

## 🔧 Fixes & Improvements

- Bedrock now properly drops itself when mined with the Bedrock Pickaxe.
- The Mace no longer performs sword-style sweep attacks.
- Optimized the Wind Charge texture for better loading.
- Refined Emerald Armor textures and models for a cleaner, more polished appearance.
- Removed the unwanted gold-colored top layer from the Emerald Armor textures.
- Rainbow Armor upgraded from a flat texture to a full 3D model, with extra detail pieces and a glossy rainbow finish (v1.5.3).
- Old builds no longer get in the way of new releases.
- **New in v1.5.6:** Density, Breach, Wind Burst and Swift Sneak enchantments are now fully functional — real smash-attack bonus damage, armor piercing, Wind Burst launch chaining, faster sneaking, and enchanted-book chest loot.

## 📦 Compatibility

- **Minecraft:** 1.16.5 (Fabric)
- **Requires:** Fabric API
- **Works with:** Sodium and Iris
